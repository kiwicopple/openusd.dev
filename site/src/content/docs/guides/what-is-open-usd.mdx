**Open USD (OUSD) is a dollar [stablecoin](/guides/concepts/#stablecoin) created by Open Standard and [issued](/guides/concepts/#issuer) by Bridge Building Inc.** It launched on September 30, 2026. [Issuer announcement](https://withbridge.com/blog/ousd-is-live-issued-by-bridge).

## Key facts

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who issues it? | Bridge Building Inc. |
| Which [networks](/guides/concepts/#network) is it on? | Base, Ethereum, Solana, and Tempo. |
| Where are the contracts? | [Open Standard's official directory](https://joinopenstandard.com/integrate/). |
| How do I integrate it? | [Choose a task](/start/) for the operation you need. |

Bridge National Trust Bank is a separate entity in organization. It is not yet operational and does not currently issue OUSD.

## Governance and reserves

Open Standard names Coinbase, Mastercard, Shopify, Stripe, and Visa as founding partners. Its [company-structure announcement](https://joinopenstandard.com/blog/company-structure-and-leadership) describes partner equity based on supply and activity, and shareholder representation on the board.

Its [launch announcement](https://joinopenstandard.com/blog/ousd-is-live) names BlackRock, Lead Bank, and other large financial institutions as [reserve](/guides/concepts/#reserves) holders and points to [Bridge's reserve portal](https://reserves.bridge.xyz/ousd) for monthly [attestations](/guides/concepts/#attestation). This reference has not reviewed an attestation report or audited the reserves.

## Integration access

A deployed token and a provider account are separate things. Confirm the operation, network, and account eligibility with your chosen provider. The [directory](/ecosystem/) records access requirements and unresolved support questions.

## Similar names

| Name | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open USD / OUSD | Open Standard's payments stablecoin, covered here. |
| [OpenUSD](https://openusd.org/release/intro.html) | Universal Scene Description, a 3D graphics framework. |
| [Origin Dollar / OUSD](https://docs.originprotocol.com/yield-bearing-tokens/origin-dollar-ousd) | A different token from Origin. |

Use the official [token address](/guides/concepts/#token-address) and network to identify a token; the ticker alone is ambiguous.

**Open USD is a [stablecoin](/guides/concepts/#stablecoin). Open Issuance is Bridge's platform for creating custom stablecoins.**

| | Open USD | Open Issuance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you use | An existing asset: OUSD. | A platform to launch and manage your own asset. |
| Main decision | Which provider and operation to integrate. | How to configure and operate a custom stablecoin. |
| Start here | [OUSD integration paths](/start/). | [Bridge's Open Issuance announcement](https://stripe.com/blog/introducing-open-issuance-from-bridge). |

Bridge confirms that **Bridge Building Inc. issues OUSD**. [Issuer announcement](https://withbridge.com/blog/ousd-is-live-issued-by-bridge).

## When custom issuance matters

Open Issuance offers choices for [networks](/guides/concepts/#network), [contract](/guides/concepts/#smart-contract) functionality, and reserve allocation. It also describes swaps among participating coins. Those platform features do not establish the configuration or availability of a particular OUSD service.

If you only need to accept, hold, or send OUSD, start with an existing provider. If you need a distinct asset, discuss issuance terms, operational responsibilities, and supported jurisdictions with Bridge.

## Development environment

Bridge's [sandbox](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/get-started/introduction/quick-start/setting-up-sandbox) simulates payment activity and webhooks without live bank or blockchain transactions. A successful simulation does not establish a working production issuance or OUSD settlement flow.
